Hi! I am Iker, a PhD student in this DTE. I hold a BSc in Economics with a Minor in Applied Mathematics from Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF), an MSc in Statistics and Operations Research from the Polytechnic University of Catalonia (UPC), an MSc in Mathematical and Computational Engineering from Universitat Rovira i Virgili (URV), and an MSc in Financial Mathematics from the University of Warwick. Alongside my MSc studies, I also worked full-time in the financial industry as a quantitative risk analyst at Deloitte and as a quantitative researcher at a cryptocurrency market-making firm.

During my studies, I have worked on mathematical and computational modelling in finance, extreme value theory and complex systems in economics, sequential modelling in machine learning, statistical machine learning, and the use of non-additive capacities in machine learning. I am now interested in uncertainty quantification and sequential decision-making in machine learning, particularly in real-world settings.
