Hi! I am Iker, a PhD student in this DTE. I graduated with a BSc in Economics with a Minor in Applied Mathematics at Universitat Pompeu Fabra (UPF), an MSc in Statistics and Operations Research at the Polytechnic University of Catalonia (UPC), an MSc in Mathematical and Computational Engineering at Universitat Rovira i Virgili (URV), and an MSc in Financial Mathematics at the University of Warwick. During my MSc student time, I also worked full-time at the financial industry as a quantitative risk analyst for Deloitte and a quantitative researcher in a cryptocurrency market-making firm.

During my studies I have worked in mathematical and computational modelling in finance, extreme value theory and complex systems for economics, sequential modelling in machine learning, statistical machine learning and the usage of non-additive capacities for machine learning. Now, I am interested in uncertainty quantification and sequential decision-making in machine learning applied to real-world settings.
